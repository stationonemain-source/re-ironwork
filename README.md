# RE Ironwork, website prototype

A seven-page design prototype for a custom forged-iron door shop.

- Home, Build a door, All 120 doors, Work, Process, The shop, Send your ticket.
- The configurator shows a rendered photograph for every combination of size, style, finish
  and glass (120 doors). The door you build travels with you between pages as a job ticket.
- Door and shop photographs are AI-generated concept renders.
- Branding, copy, projects, prices, lead times and contact details are placeholders.
- The quote form does not send anything.

Static site, no build step for visitors. Source partials live outside this repo.
