One shop, one forge, in Norman.

Everything on this site is drawn, forged, finished and hung by the same small crew.

"I would rather turn work away than send it to somebody I cannot stand next to at the anvil. Everything that leaves here, I have had my hands on."

Ruben Torres

Founder

What we make

  • Entry doorsSingle, double, arched and flush, with thermally broken frames.
  • Driveway and garden gatesSwing and slide, with the operator and loops sorted before cutting.
  • Stairs and railingsBalustrades and balconies, templated on site.
  • Steel windowsSlim profiles with true divided lites.
  • RestorationPeriod ironwork, when it is worth saving.

What we do not

  • Catalogue doorsIf it comes in a crate, a fabricator will beat our price.
  • Cast ornamentEvery scroll here is hammered, so no two match exactly.
  • Subcontracted installsThe crew that builds it is the crew that hangs it.
  • Rush jobs under ten weeksForging does not compress, and we will not pretend it does.
