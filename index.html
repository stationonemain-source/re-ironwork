Is the door in the configurator the door I get?
It is the starting point. The pictures are real renders of each style, finish and glass combination, and the shop builds to a full-size drawing of your opening. Proportions change with your opening; the ironwork, finish and glass do not.
Is forged iron actually different from what a fabricator sells?
Yes, and it is visible. Forged elements taper and thicken where the metal was moved under a hammer. Cold-bent stock is a constant thickness the whole way round, and cast ornament repeats identically across every door in a catalogue.
How does payment work?
A design retainer for the drawings, credited in full against the build. Half on approval of the drawing, the balance on completion before install. No deposit until you have signed a full-size elevation.
What about rust and Oklahoma weather?
Everything is primed and sealed, and exterior work gets a finish specified for west and south exposure. Maintenance sheet included, ninety-day return visit, and exterior re-oiling at cost after year one.
Do you do repairs and restoration?
Yes, on period ironwork especially. Send photographs and a rough measurement and we will say honestly whether it is worth restoring.
